Mega Press - Delete your data

App: Mega Press: Crush Everything (Google Play)
Developer: Jungle Pixel Studio, Bogotá, Colombia

Mega Press has no accounts and we do not keep your personal data on our own servers. This page explains how to delete the data related to the game and what is deleted or kept.

1. Delete your game progress

Your progress (levels, coins, upgrades, unlocked presses) and settings (sound, music, vibration, language) are saved only on your device.

  1. Open your device Settings > Apps > Mega Press.
  2. Tap Storage and then Clear data. Uninstalling the game also deletes it.

This data is deleted right away and cannot be recovered.

2. Delete or reset your advertising ID

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect your IP address (from which an approximate location can be inferred), how you interact with the game and its ads, diagnostic information and device identifiers such as the advertising ID.

  1. Open your device Settings > Google > Ads (on some devices: Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  2. Tap Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID.

After this, new ad activity is no longer linked to your old advertising ID.

3. Ask us to delete your data

  1. Write to junglepixel2024@gmail.com with the subject "Mega Press - Delete my data".
  2. Tell us what you want deleted. We answer within 30 days.

4. What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: your game progress and settings, as soon as you clear the game's data or uninstall it.
  • Kept by Google: advertising data that Google AdMob has already collected is kept and deleted by Google according to its own data retention policy.
  • Kept by us: nothing, except your email request, which we keep only as long as we need to answer it.

More details are in our Privacy Policy. If you play Mega Press on CrazyGames, that platform handles your data there under the CrazyGames Privacy Policy.
