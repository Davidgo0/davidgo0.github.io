Mega Press - Privacy Policy

Developer: Jungle Pixel Studio, Bogotá, Colombia
Effective date: November 7, 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you play Mega Press (the "Game") on Android. If you have any question, write to junglepixel2024@gmail.com.

1. Information the Game itself stores

The Game does not ask for your name, email, phone number or any account. Your progress (current level, coins, upgrades, the press you have unlocked and settings such as sound, music, vibration and language) is saved only on your device. We do not receive or store this information on our own servers.

The Game does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, files or precise location.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location can be inferred), device and app information, and how you interact with ads.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the Privacy button in the Game's settings. On any Android device you can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Learn more in Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

3. Game engine

The Game is built with the Unity engine. Unity may process limited technical data, such as device model, operating system version and crash information, as described in the Unity Privacy Policy.

4. How the information is used and shared

Information is used only to run the Game, show and measure ads, fix problems and prevent fraud. We do not sell personal information. Data is shared only with the service providers named above, acting for us, or with authorities when the law requires it. These providers may process data in countries other than yours under their own safeguards.

5. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

Your progress stays on your device until you clear the Game's data or uninstall it. Advertising data is kept by Google according to its own policies; you can reset your advertising ID at any time. To ask about or request deletion of any information related to you, email junglepixel2024@gmail.com. Step-by-step instructions are on the Delete your data page.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information. Contact us at the email above and we will answer within a reasonable time.

8. Web version

If you play Mega Press on CrazyGames, that platform handles saving and advertising there under the CrazyGames Privacy Policy.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game changes. The new version will be published on this page with its effective date.
